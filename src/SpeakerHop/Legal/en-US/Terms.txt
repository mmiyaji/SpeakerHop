SpeakerHop — Terms of use
Effective: 2026-11-01

1. Purpose
SpeakerHop is a desktop application for changing the Windows default audio output, adjusting volume and controlling output/default microphone mute. Obtain the app from official distribution sources and use it on a PC you are authorized to control.

2. Effects of operations
Configured global shortcuts operate while other applications are active. Changing the output or unmuting may affect meetings, calls and audio currently playing. Microphone controls target the Windows Multimedia default input; they do not uniformly change separately configured Communications devices.
